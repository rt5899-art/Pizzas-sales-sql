# Pizza Sales Performance Analysis

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project delivers an end-to-end data analytics solution designed to evaluate transactional pizza sales data, extract key performance metrics, and uncover consumer behavior patterns. Utilizing robust SQL architectures and dynamic reporting structures, the analysis bridges the gap between raw data and business intelligence, tracking sales performance across product categories, sizes, and operational timelines.

---

## 2. Tools & Technologies

* **Database Management System:** MS SQL Server / MySQL (Data ingestion, structuring, and relational integrity)
* **SQL Techniques:** Multi-table JOINS, Aggregate Functions (`SUM`, `COUNT`, `AVG`), Advanced Subqueries, Data Type Casting (`CAST`), and Date/Time Manipulation functions (`DATENAME`, `MONTH`, `DATEPART`)

---

### Key Business Insights

* **Peak Demand Operations:** Peak ordering volume concentrates heavily around weekends (**Friday** and **Saturday** evenings) and during daily lunchtime hours (**12:00 PM – 1:00 PM**).
* **Inventory Dominance:** Large pizzas drive the highest revenue share, commanding approximately **45.89%** of total sales distributions.
* **Category Performance:** The **Classic** pizza category represents the highest performance footprint, generating **26.91%** of overall business revenue.
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** **The Thai Chicken Pizza**, **The Barbecue Chicken Pizza**, and **The Classic Deluxe Pizza** stand as the premier top 3 products by revenue.
* **Lowest Revenue Margin:** **The Brie Carre Pizza** underperforms significantly, marking its place among the bottom 5 revenue-generating items.

---

## 4. Strategic Recommendations

* **Busiest Timelines ➔ Targeted Staffing & Preparation** Optimize labor schedules and pre-prep workflows specifically on Fridays, Saturdays, and daily from 11:30 AM to 1:30 PM to handle peak transaction spikes without increasing customer wait times.

* **High Volume Categories ➔ Strategic Menu Bundling** Leverage high-performing products like the Classic Deluxe or Thai Chicken in promotional "Bundle Offers" (e.g., paired with slow-moving appetizers or sides) to maximize the average order value (AOV).

* **Product Disparity ➔ Menu Optimization** Rework recipes, adjust pricing tiers, or completely phase out consistently underperforming menu items like the Brie Carre Pizza to minimize waste and streamline kitchen operations.

* **Size Demand ➔ Targeted Supply Chain Allocations** Maintain aggressive stock and inventory thresholds for Large-size pizza crusts and corresponding ingredients to prevent stockouts during high-demand weekend rushes.
